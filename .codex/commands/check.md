Use the `solution-coach` skill for: $ARGUMENTS

Locate the exercise contract or current guided-project milestone and its related learning material, run the narrowest available checks, assess each in-scope requirement with evidence, and provide the smallest useful next hint. Do not assess future milestones, silently edit the learner's work, or reveal a complete replacement unless the learner explicitly asks after attempting the hints. Do not invoke subagents automatically.
